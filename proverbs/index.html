    How it works

    A notebook to leaf through, one saying at a time.

    A saying a day

    Every day the app opens on a saying you haven’t read yet. If you like, a notification reminds you at the time you choose.

    Five languages

    Swipe the phrase left and right to see it in English, Italian, Spanish, French and German.

    Meaning and origin

    Tap the phrase to open the explanation, with the meaning and the story of each expression, language by language.

    The notebook

    365 sayings to leaf through, 25 themes, your favourites and a search that finds phrases in all five languages.

    Photo of the day

    Take a photo, or pick one from your gallery, with the phrase on it: choose from nine frames, then save it or share it.

    Report an error

    Found a mistake? Report it straight from the app: the texts get better thanks to the people who read them.

    Share

    The right phrase, at the right moment.

    One tap and the saying you are reading becomes an image to send, in the language you prefer.

    Free, with no ads.

    All 365 sayings are open from day one. If you like the app, you can buy me a coffee from inside it: it’s a gift and doesn’t unlock anything.

    Coming soon to Google Play

    For Android, and soon iPhone. English, Italiano, Español, Français, Deutsch.

    About the texts

    The phrases, meanings and origins were gathered and written with the help of artificial intelligence, then checked against dictionaries and public sources. The origins of many sayings are uncertain or disputed: those given here are the most widely accepted explanations, not established facts.
