Idioms & Proverbs

Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

In short. The Idioms & Proverbs app has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Almost everything stays on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone is what you choose to send: an error report (with your email only if you type it) and, if you buy me a coffee, the payment, which is handled by Google Play or the App Store.

Who is responsible

The data controller is the developer of the Idioms & Proverbs app (devtbe). For any question about privacy, write to dev.tbe.01@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your phone

The app saves some information only in your phone’s memory, so that it works the way you expect: the app language, the light or dark theme, your favourite sayings, the sayings you have already read, the time of the “A saying a day” reminder and your sound and vibration settings. This data is not sent to anyone and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone’s settings.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only in “Photo of the day”, when you open it. If you pick a photo from your gallery instead, the app opens only that photo, to put the saying on it. The photo with the saying stays on your phone: it is saved to your gallery only if you tap “Save”, and sent to others only if you tap “Share” and choose where to send it. The app does not upload photos to any server.

Notifications

The “A saying a day” reminder is a local notification, scheduled on your phone. It does not go through any server and you can turn it off at any time in the app’s settings.

Error reports

If you use “Report an error” and confirm sending it, the app sends:

  • the saying and the sentence the report is about, and its language;
  • the text you wrote;
  • your email, only if you chose to enter it;
  • the app language and the app version.

The report is stored in a Google spreadsheet belonging to the controller, through the Google Apps Script service, and the controller is notified by email. This data is used only to check and correct the texts and, if you left your email, to reply to you. It is not used for any other purpose or passed on to third parties.

The legal basis is your consent, which you give by sending the report. Reports are kept as long as needed to check them and correct the texts, and in any case no longer than 24 months. Google acts as a service provider: its own processing is covered by Google’s privacy policy.

“Buy me a coffee”

If you choose to buy me a coffee, the payment goes through the store you installed the app from: Google Play’s billing system on Android, or Apple’s App Store on iPhone. Payment data is processed by Google or Apple: the controller does not receive your card number or any other payment details, only the transaction information that the store makes available to developers. Google’s privacy policy or Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Ads and tracking

The app shows no ads and uses no analytics, profiling or tracking tools.

Children

The app is meant for people aged 13 and over (a few idioms contain mild swear words). It does not ask for personal data to work; children under 14 should not enter their email in error reports.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you can ask to access the data about you, to correct or delete it, to restrict its processing and to withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to dev.tbe.01@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the country where you live.

Delete your data

To ask for the deletion of the data you sent with the Idioms & Proverbs app (“Idioms & Proverbs: 5 languages” on Google Play and the App Store):

  1. write to dev.tbe.01@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion – Idioms & Proverbs”;
  2. give the email you left in your report or, if you didn’t leave one, the text and approximate date of your report;
  3. you will receive a confirmation within 30 days.

What is deleted: all reports about you (text, the saying and language they refer to, app version and your email, if you entered it). No copy is kept after deletion. Even without a request, reports are deleted after 24 months at the latest.

Data that stays on your phone (favourites, settings, photos) is deleted by you, by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your phone’s settings. Payment data for the coffees is handled by Google Play or the App Store: Google’s privacy policy or Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
